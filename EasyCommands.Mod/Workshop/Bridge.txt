[h1]EasyCommands Bridge[/h1]
The small programmable-block script for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3823650249]EasyCommands Mod[/url]. Write readable automation commands in your PB's Custom Data while the mod runs the EasyCommands interpreter.

[h2]Requires EasyCommands Mod[/h2]
Subscribe to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3823650249]EasyCommands Mod[/url] and add it to your world's Mods list. The bridge cannot run on its own, and subscribing to a required mod does not enable it in your world.

[h2]Get started[/h2]
[list]
[*]Enable EasyCommands Mod in your world, along with Experimental mode and in-game scripts.
[*]Open a powered programmable block's editor and load EasyCommands Bridge from Workshop.
[*]Put your EasyCommands program in the block's Custom Data, then click Run.
[/list]
Moving from standalone EasyCommands? Keep your existing Custom Data program; this bridge replaces the larger script in the PB editor.

[h2]Write your first program[/h2]
EasyCommands lets you control blocks, work with variables, run functions and threads, respond to triggers, and communicate between grids using IGC. Each PB keeps its own program and runtime.

[h2]Settings and output[/h2]
Set updateFrequency and logLevel near the top of the bridge. The update interval controls both incremental parsing and program execution. INFO includes parsing and execution status; SCRIPT_ONLY shows your program's output and errors. Output appears in the PB's output panel, and LCD commands work as usual.

The PB must remain powered and enabled for scheduled execution. Recompiling the bridge or restarting the world resets variables and running threads.

If the PB reports a missing or incompatible mod, enable the matching mod, reload the world and click Run again. When a release requires a new bridge, reload it from Workshop into your PB; subscribing alone does not update code already loaded into a block.
